## Contracts / ADR
Identity is an exact unordered set of proportional rational members. Original spelling/order and repeated members remain metadata. Repetition does not increase distinct JI cardinality. Reciprocal chords remain distinct identities except self-dual cases. Identity is not reduced by octave or period.
Projection uses the existing `engine.mapRatio` and `pitch.coordinateCents`. Basis status, per-note tuning quality, reduced cardinality and finite-scale membership are independent. Exact rank-2 period equivalence discards the integer period coordinate and retains generator coordinate; EDO tuning alone does not change formal rank. No epsilon establishes identity or membership. Cents are for tuning, display and approximate substitutes only.
Scale membership consumes `engine.scale` and `pitch.noteCoordinate`; mode is explicit. Occurrences include exact assignments and stable identifiers, independent of voicing and board. Missing coordinates refer to unique mapped pitch classes, avoiding collision inflation. Physical placement uses existing `chordVoicings` and `chordPlacements` through adapters; it cannot alter occurrence truth.
Current feature boundary: sole version 2 catalogue engine, enabled for every user. Nick authorized default adoption and legacy retirement after the v87/v88 preview checkpoints. The historical stage entries below record the original gates; the final retirement entry supersedes their pending approval status. Existing sessions retain their envelope and old data, with a narrow compatibility adapter. No new services or dependencies.
